## 🎯 Placement Focus

The most important topics for interviews are:

* **Processes & Threads**
* **CPU Scheduling**
* **Synchronization**
* **Mutex & Semaphore**
* **Deadlocks**
* **Memory Management**
* **Paging & Virtual Memory**
* **System Calls**
* **File Systems**

Special focus is given to **conceptual understanding, comparisons, interview questions, and numerical problems**.

---

## 🧠 Learning Approach

Each topic is explained using:

1. **Simple Explanation** — Understand the idea first.
2. **Real-World Examples** — Connect concepts with practical situations.
3. **Technical Explanation** — Learn the terminology expected in interviews.
4. **Placement Questions** — Practice commonly asked interview questions.
5. **Quick Revision** — Review important points before interviews.
